![[Pasted_image_20260112154115.png]]

Buff_is_an_easy_difficulty_Windows_machine_that_features_an_instance_of_Gym_Management_System_1.0._This_is_found_to_suffer_from_an_unauthenticated_remote_code_execution_vulnerability._Enumeration_of_the_internal_network_reveals_a_service_running_at_port_8888._The_installation_file_for_this_service_can_be_found_on_disk,_allowing_us_to_debug_it_locally._We_can_perform_port_forwarding_in_order_to_make_the_service_available_and_exploit_it.
